# Privacy Policy for Ente Wayanad

**Effective Date:** March 2026  
**Last Updated:** March 2026  

This Privacy Policy applies to the **Ente Wayanad** mobile application ("the App"), developed and maintained by **Corbit Technologies** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information You Provide:
* **Account Information:** When you register or log in, we may collect your phone number, display name, and basic profile details via Firebase Authentication.
* **User-Generated Content & Listings:** If you submit a business, emergency contact, classified post, or listing, we collect details such as your business name, contact numbers, address/panchayat, and uploaded photos.

### B. Device & Technical Information:
* **Device Identifiers & Push Tokens:** We collect device tokens using Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver breaking news, community announcements, and relevant updates.
* **App Performance & Crash Data:** Standard anonymous crash analytics and diagnostic logs may be collected to help us debug issues and improve app stability.

### C. Permissions Required:
* **Camera & Photo Library:** Used solely when you take or choose a picture to attach to a listing, profile, or classified ad.
* **Internet & Network Access:** Required to fetch live directory data, news, and cloud database records.
* **Notifications:** To deliver emergency alerts, local updates, and important announcements.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
* To provide, maintain, and improve the Ente Wayanad app and directory services.
* To verify and publish community listings, local businesses, and emergency services.
* To send timely push notifications regarding local news, emergency updates, or administrative alerts.
* To prevent fraud, abuse, and maintain overall platform safety.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

* **Public Directory Listings:** Contact numbers and addresses you voluntarily publish as part of a public directory listing (e.g., shops, services, blood donors) are made visible to other users of the app as intended by the listing.
* **Third-Party Service Providers:** We use trusted Google Firebase services (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Firebase Cloud Messaging) to securely store and process data in compliance with standard security protocols.
* **Legal Requirements:** We will only disclose personal data if required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities in India.
* **No Sale of Data:** We **do not sell, rent, or trade** your personal information to third parties or advertisers.

---

## 4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures, including HTTPS encryption in transit and Firebase security rules on our database, to safeguard your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

---

## 5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy:
* You may update or delete your user listings at any time through the app.
* If you wish to delete your account and associated personal data completely, you can contact us at **support@wayanadcity.com** with your registered phone number, and we will process the deletion request within 7 business days.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Ente Wayanad is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
