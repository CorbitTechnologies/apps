# QuqSearch

## Privacy Policy
**Last Updated: August 30, 2026**

At QuqSearch, your privacy is a priority. This policy outlines how we handle your data.

**1. Data We Collect:**
- **Account Information:** Name, email, and phone number when you register.
- **Listing Data:** Information, images, and locations you provide when posting an ad.
- **Communications:** Chat messages sent through our platform to facilitate transactions.
- **Usage Data:** Device information, IP addresses, and app interactions to improve our service.

**2. How We Use Your Data:**
- To provide, maintain, and personalize the QuqSearch marketplace.
- To facilitate communication between buyers and sellers.
- To send important notifications, updates, and promotional offers (which you can opt out of).
- To detect and prevent fraud, spam, or abuse.

**3. Data Sharing:**
- We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
- Your public profile (name) and listing details are visible to other users.
- We may share data with trusted service providers (e.g., hosting, analytics) who assist in operating our app, strictly under confidentiality agreements.

**4. Your Rights:**
- You can access, update, or delete your account and listings at any time through the app settings.
- If you request account deletion, your personal data will be permanently removed, except where required by law.
