# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: May 10, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Phinverse Finance ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App") and related services.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

- **Account Registration Information**: Name, email address, phone number, and password

- **Profile Information**: Personal preferences, account settings, and user profile data
- **Payment Information**: Banking details and payment method information
- **Communication**: Messages, support requests, and feedback you provide

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, and device settings
- **Usage Information**: Features accessed, actions taken, and time spent on various App sections

- **Location Information**: Approximate location based on IP address (with your consent)
- **Cookies and Similar Technologies**: We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance functionality

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

- **App Functionality**: Providing, maintaining, and improving our services
- **Account Management**: Registering, authenticating, and managing your account
- **Financial Services**: Processing transactions, managing investments, and generating financial reports
- **Communication**: Sending transactional updates, security alerts, and customer support
- **Analytics**: Understanding user behavior and improving the App experience
- **Security**: Detecting, preventing, and addressing fraud and security issues
- **Legal Compliance**: Meeting regulatory requirements and legal obligations
- **Marketing**: Sending promotional content (with your consent)

## 4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

### 4.1 We Do NOT Share Your Information For:
- Marketing or commercial purposes without explicit consent
- Selling your data to third parties

### 4.2 We May Share Information With:

**Service Providers**: Third-party vendors who assist us (payment processors, cloud hosts, analytics providers) under strict confidentiality agreements.

**Financial Institutions**: Banks, brokerages, and investment platforms necessary to execute your transactions.

**Legal Requirements**: Law enforcement, government agencies, or courts when required by law or valid legal process.

**Business Transfers**: In case of merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

**Your Consent**: With your explicit permission for specific purposes.

## 5. Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your information:

- **Encryption**: End-to-end encryption for sensitive data transmission
- **Secure Authentication**: Multi-factor authentication options
- **Secure Storage**: Encrypted data storage with access controls
- **Regular Security Audits**: Periodic security assessments and vulnerability testing
- **Staff Training**: Employee privacy and security training
- **Incident Response**: Documented procedures for handling data breaches

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to:

- Provide our services

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, subject to legal retention requirements.
