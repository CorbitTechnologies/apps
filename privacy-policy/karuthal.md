# Privacy Policy for KARUTHAL**Effective Date:** October 17, 2026**Last Updated:** October 17, 2026Welcome to **KARUTHAL** ("KARUTHAL", "we", "our", or "us"), a secure digital legacy platform for wills, vault, and end-of-life planning operated by Corbit Technologies. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a secure experience for individuals planning their digital and physical legacy.This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile application and related services ("Services").---## 1. Information We Collect### A. Information You Provide Directly
**Account Information:** Full name, mobile number (verified via one-time password / OTP), and residential address.
**Profile:** Profile photograph, display details, and account preferences.
**Security Credentials:** A passkey/PIN and security question & answer, stored only in hashed or encrypted form — your raw PIN or answer is never retained by us in readable form.
**Will & Legacy Data:** Digital will contents, assets and their documents, legal will details, posthumous instructions, payment reminders, and nominated relationships (family, friends, or trusted contacts you add).
**Digital Vault Documents:** Personal documents and images you choose to store in your private vault, encrypted before storage.
**Community Content:** Wiki posts, comments, and images you publish to the KARUTHAL knowledge community (public or private).
**Communications:** Support requests and in-app notifications.
### B. Information Collected Automatically / Stored Locally
**Device Session Data:** Customer ID, session tokens, hashed PIN, and preferences stored locally on your device via secure local storage to keep you signed in and enable offline access.
**Encrypted Local Cache:** Cached vault/will asset metadata, encrypted on-device.
**Usage & Engagement Data:** Life check-in responses, notification interactions, and feature usage within the app.
### C. Device Permissions Requested
**Camera Access:** To capture profile photos, vault documents, asset images, and wiki post images.
**Photo Library / Storage Access:** To select and upload documents, images, and media files.
**Location Access (When In Use):** To locate nearby certified KARUTHAL agents for Physical Will services, sorted by proximity.
**Internet Access:** Required for authentication, synchronisation, and secure cloud operations.
---## 2. How We Use Your InformationWe use the collected information for the following purposes:
**Account & Security:** Verifying your mobile number via OTP, maintaining your session, and securing access with your passkey/PIN using one-way hashing and encryption.
**Will & Legacy Management:** Creating, storing, encrypting, and rendering your digital will, legal will, assets, vault documents, posthumous instructions, and payment reminders.
**Life Check-In & Alerts:** Operating the periodic life check-in feature and issuing death notifications and reminders to your nominated contacts in line with your configured settings.
**Agent Matching:** Showing nearby certified agents and enabling Physical Will service requests, including sharing your name, contact, and address with an agent only when you initiate a request.
**Backup & Export:** Generating PDF will documents and, at your option, uploading them to **your own personal Google Drive** (folder /KARUTHAL_VAULT) — we do not centrally store your vault files on our servers.
**Community Features:** Publishing your wiki posts according to your chosen public/private visibility.
**Support:** Responding to your queries and service requests.
---## 3. How We Share Your InformationWe do **not** sell your personal data to third parties. We share your information only in the following operational circumstances:
**With Assigned Agents:** When you request a Physical Will service, your name, phone number, and residential address are shared with the certified agent assigned to your request so they can coordinate the meeting.
**With Nominated Contacts:** Death notifications and instructions are delivered to contacts you explicitly nominate, strictly according to your check-in settings.
**With Other Users:** Wiki posts you publish as "public" are visible to the KARUTHAL community according to your chosen visibility setting.
**Service Providers & Sub-processors:**
  * **Google Firebase:** For authentication (Firebase Auth), database (Cloud Firestore), and app data storage.  * **MSG91:** For sending one-time passwords (OTP) to verify your mobile number.  * **Google Sign-In / Google Drive API:** For your optional backup of encrypted will/vault documents to your personal Google Drive (limited to the drive.file scope — we can only access files created by this app).
**Legal & Safety Disclosures:** If required by law, subpoena, or to protect the safety, rights, and security of KARUTHAL, our users, or the public.
---## 4. Data Security & Retention
**Encryption:** Will and vault data is encrypted using strong encryption (AES-256-GCM) with keys derived from your passkey before it leaves your device. Communications are protected by TLS/HTTPS in transit, and data at rest is protected via Google Cloud and strict Firebase Security Rules.
**Zero-Central Vault Storage:** Your digital vault documents are stored in your personal Google Drive, not on our servers.
**Data Retention:** We retain your personal information as long as your account remains active. Upon account deletion, your profile, will records, and vault metadata are securely purged or anonymised, except where legal retention is required. Files in your personal Google Drive remain under your control and can be deleted by you at any time.
**Local Storage:** Sensitive credentials on your device are stored hashed or encrypted, never in plain text.
---## 5. Your Rights & ChoicesYou have the following controls over your data:
**Access & Edit:** Update your profile, will, assets, relationships, instructions, and preferences directly within the app.
**Account Deletion:** Request permanent account deletion via **Settings** or by contacting support. Deleting your account does not delete files already backed up to your personal Google Drive — those remain yours to manage.
**Backup Control:** Connect or disconnect Google Drive backup anytime; backups are created only with your explicit action.
**Visibility Control:** Choose whether wiki posts are public or private, and manage global visibility of your data from **Privacy & Data Protection** in your profile.
**Permissions Control:** Revoke camera, photo library, or location permissions through your mobile device settings.
---## 6. Children's PrivacyKARUTHAL handles estate and legacy planning and is intended for adults who are **18 years of age or older** and legally competent to make a will under applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we discover a user under 18 has registered, we will promptly delete their account.---## 7.
