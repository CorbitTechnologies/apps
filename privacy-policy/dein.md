# Privacy Policy for DE.IN

**Last Updated:** September 23, 2026

Corbit Technologies ("we," "our," or "us") operates the DE.IN mobile application (the "App") in collaboration with DE.IN INTERIO EXTERIO. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

By downloading, installing, or using DE.IN, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

To provide a personalized interior design lookbook, physical space measurement guidance, real-time cost estimation, and project consultation experience, we may collect several types of information:

#### Personal Data
When you complete your profile or submit a design inquiry, you may provide personally identifiable information, including:
- Name
- Phone number (10-digit mobile number)
- Place / City (serviceable location for physical site verification and installation)
- Preferred consultation timeslot and design notes

#### Project and Architectural Data
To calculate your space requirements, material specifications, and indicative cost estimates, we may collect and process project-related information that you voluntarily configure, such as:
- Room types, wall dimensions, and measurement units (Centimetres, Metres, Inches, Feet, Running Feet)
- Selected modular designs, layout types, finish palettes, and add-on accessories
- Indicative budget estimates and calculated square footage
- Saved design preferences and multi-room project cart items

#### Usage and Device Data
We may automatically collect certain technical information when you access the App, including:
- An anonymous unique device identifier (`device_uuid`) generated on your device
- Your device's operating system (Android / iOS), app version, and IP address
- In-app activity logs, lookbook pages viewed, and catalog interaction events
- Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration tokens for push notifications
- App crash reports and performance diagnostics

#### Financial Data Disclaimer
We do not collect, process, or store credit card details, debit card numbers, bank account information, or digital payment credentials. The App does not contain a digital payment gateway. All commercial agreements, advance payments, and milestone settlements are conducted strictly offline directly with the showroom.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for various purposes, including to:
- Provide, maintain, and improve the core functionalities of DE.IN
- Calculate and display real-time indicative budget estimates and material requirements based on your dimensions
- Store and pre-fill your contact information locally on your device for fast 1-tap inquiries
- Transmit your design inquiry dossiers (`#DE-XXXX`) to authorized interior designers and showroom personnel
- Coordinate physical site inspections, space audits, and personalized 3D design consultations
- Connect you directly with design specialists via phone, email, or WhatsApp support
- Manage and track your submitted project inquiries and quotation status
- Deliver essential project updates, consultation notifications, and design inspiration broadcasts
- Analyze usage patterns to enhance lookbook visual rendering, app speed, and user experience

---

### 3. How Your Information Is Shared

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or architectural project data to third parties. We may share your information only under the following circumstances:

- **With Design Consultants & Showroom Teams:** We share your submitted project specifications and contact details with DE.IN interior design consultants and showroom specialists to prepare 3D layouts, schedule site measurements, and generate formal quotations.
- **With Service Providers:** We may share data with trusted third-party cloud service providers (such as Google Cloud Firestore for database storage and Firebase for media delivery and push notifications) to perform infrastructure services on our behalf, subject to strict confidentiality agreements.
- **For Third-Party Communications:** When you choose to initiate a WhatsApp consultation, your inquiry reference code and project title are passed to WhatsApp (Meta Platforms, Inc.) via universal deep links to start your conversation. We do not monitor, read, or scrape your WhatsApp messages.
- **For Legal Reasons:** We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, or in the good faith belief that such action is necessary to comply with legal obligations, enforce our policies, or protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or the public.
- **Business Transfers:** If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as a business asset.

---

### 4. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect the safety of your personal and project information. All network communication between the App and cloud services is encrypted using TLS (Transport Layer Security). Dynamic dimension and budget computations are performed client-side on your device. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is entirely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute data security.

---

### 5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal and inquiry data only for as long as is necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy.

- **Local Device Storage:** Your profile name, mobile number, place, and submitted inquiry history are stored locally on your device in local cache. You can update or clear this data at any time by editing your profile within the App or by clearing the App's storage/cache in your device settings.
- **Cloud Inquiry Records:** Submitted inquiry dossiers are retained for showroom quotation records and consultation continuity. If you wish to request the permanent deletion of your inquiry records from our systems, you may contact us using the details provided below.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy

DE.IN is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we discover that a child has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such data from our systems promptly.

---

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the App and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. You are advised to review this policy periodically for any changes.
