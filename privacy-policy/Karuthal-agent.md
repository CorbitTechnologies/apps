# Privacy Policy for KARUTHAL Agent**Effective Date:** October 17, 2026**Last Updated:** October 17, 2026Welcome to **KARUTHAL Agent** ("KARUTHAL Agent", "we", "our", or "us"), the certified legal will facilitator application operated by Corbit Technologies. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a secure experience for our field agents who facilitate Physical Will service requests for customers.This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile application and related services ("Services").---## 1. Information We Collect### A. Information You Provide Directly
**Account Information:** Email address and password used to sign in to your pre-registered agent account (authenticated via Firebase Authentication).
**Agent Profile:** Full name, email address, phone number, designation, district/region, and profile photograph.
**Profile Photograph:** Images captured with your camera or selected from your photo gallery, uploaded to secure cloud storage.
**Ratings & Reviews:** Feedback and star ratings submitted by customers who were assisted by you.
### B. Information Collected Automatically / In Course of Service
**Service Request Data:** Customer name, customer phone number, and customer residential address (displayed to you only for the purpose of completing an assigned Physical Will service request), along with request status, meeting timestamps, and facilitation fee details.
**Session Data:** A local sign-in session identifier and your account email are stored on your device to keep you logged in securely.
**Usage & Engagement Data:** Request actions performed by you, including contacting the customer, scheduling meetings, marking requests as completed, and fee collection status.
### C. Device Permissions Requested
**Camera Access:** To capture your profile photograph.
**Photo Library / Storage Access:** To select and upload a profile photograph from your device.
**Location Access (When In Use):** To support coordination of field will services in your area.
**Internet Access:** Required for authentication, data synchronisation, and cloud storage.
---## 2. How We Use Your InformationWe use the collected information for the following purposes:
**Service Delivery:** Assigning, communicating, and completing Physical Will service requests for customers in your designated area.
**Agent Account Management:** Authenticating your sign-in session and maintaining your agent profile.
**Profile & Reputation:** Displaying your name, designation, district, photograph, and customer ratings within the platform.
**Field Coordination:** Providing customer contact details and address information strictly as needed to perform an assigned service request.
**Platform Security & Verification:** Preventing fraud, protecting accounts, and enforcing platform policies.
**Support:** Responding to your queries and support requests.
---## 3. How We Share Your InformationWe do **not** sell your personal data to third parties. We share your information only in the following operational circumstances:
**With Customers:** Your name, phone number, designation, and rating are visible to customers assigned to you so they can coordinate the service.
**With the Platform Operator:** Your profile, activity, and review data are visible to KARUTHAL platform administrators for verification, quality, and operational purposes.
**Service Providers & Sub-processors:**
  * **Google Firebase:** For authentication (Firebase Auth), database (Cloud Firestore), and profile photo storage (Cloud Storage).
**Legal & Safety Disclosures:** If required by law, subpoena, or to protect the safety, rights, and security of KARUTHAL Agent, our users, or the public.
---## 4. Data Security & Retention
**Data Protection:** We implement industry-standard encryption (TLS/HTTPS in transit and encryption at rest via Google Cloud) and strict access controls through Firebase Security Rules.
**Data Retention:** We retain your personal information as long as your agent account remains active. Upon deactivation, your agent profile and associated activity records are securely purged or anonymised, except where legal retention is required.
**Local Storage:** Your signed-in session is stored locally on your device only; your password is never stored on the device.
---## 5. Your Rights & ChoicesYou have the following controls over your data:
**Access & Edit:** Update your profile, designation, district, and photograph directly within the app settings.
**Account Deletion:** Request permanent account deletion or deactivation by contacting agent support.
**Permissions Control:** Revoke camera, photo library, location, or notification permissions through your mobile device settings.
---## 6. Children's PrivacyKARUTHAL Agent is designed for certified professional agents who are **18 years of age or older**. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we discover that a minor has registered as an agent, we will promptly delete their account.---## 7. Changes to This Privacy PolicyWe may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and notify users via in-app notifications or email for material updates.---## 8.
