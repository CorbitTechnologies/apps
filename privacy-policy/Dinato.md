# Privacy Policy for Dinato

**Last Updated: July 8, 2026**

Corbit Technologies ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Dinato mobile application and platform (the "App"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App as a customer (ordering food) or as a home chef (preparing food).

By downloading, installing, or using Dinato, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

To provide a seamless, secure, and personalized home-food booking and delivery experience, we collect several types of information:

### Personal Data
When you register an account, order food, or register as a home chef, we may collect personally identifiable information, including:
*   **For Customers:** Name, phone number, email address, profile picture, and delivery addresses.
*   **For Home Chefs (Cooks):** Name, phone number, email address, kitchen name, profile picture, business address, bank account details for payouts, and government-issued identification (ID) uploads for chef verification.

### Location Data
To match customers with nearby home chefs and calculate distance-based delivery/pickup availability, we collect:
*   Precise or approximate geographical location (latitude and longitude) of your device, subject to your explicit permission.

### Transaction and Payment Data
To facilitate payment processing, we collect details related to your orders:
*   Items ordered, portions, custom instructions, transaction amounts, and payment status.
*   We use third-party payment gateways (such as Razorpay) to process payments securely. We do not store full credit card numbers or sensitive payment credentials on our servers.

### Usage and Device Data
We automatically collect diagnostic and usage logs when you access the App:
*   Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and IP address.
*   In-app activity logs, features accessed, page views, and crash reports.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for various purposes, including to:
*   Provide, operate, and maintain the Dinato platform for ordering and preparing home-cooked meals.
*   Determine your geographical location to display local home kitchens nearby.
*   Verify the identity and credentials of home chefs to maintain quality and safety standards.
*   Process orders, manage payments, and coordinate meal pickups or deliveries.
*   Calculate and pay out earnings to home chefs.
*   Respond to customer support requests and address issues or feedback.
*   Send administrative updates, order status notifications, and policy changes.

---

## 3. How Your Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal or transactional data to third parties. We share information only under the following circumstances:

*   **Between Customers and Home Chefs:** To fulfill orders, we share necessary details (such as customer name, delivery address, and contact number) with the assigned home chef.
*   **With Service Providers:** We share data with reliable third-party service providers (such as Google Maps API for geocoding, database hosting, SMS verification, and payment gateways) to run the service.
*   **For Legal Reasons:** We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, or in the good faith belief that such action is necessary to comply with legal processes or protect the safety of our users.
*   **Business Transfers:** In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, user database records may be transferred as a business asset.

---

## 4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect the safety of your personal and transactional information. However, please note that no transmission method over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal, location, and chef validation data only for as long as is necessary to fulfill the operational and compliance purposes outlined in this policy. 

You may request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by accessing your Profile settings in the App or by contacting us using the email below.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Dinato is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will immediately delete it from our systems.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any updates by posting the revised version within the App and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of the policy.
