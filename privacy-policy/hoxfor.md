# Privacy Policy for Hoxfor: Job Search & Network

**Effective Date:** August 17, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 17, 2026  

Welcome to **Hoxfor: Job Search & Network** ("Hoxfor", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a secure experience for job seekers, recruiters, employers, and professional agencies on our platform.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile application and related services ("Services").

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide Directly
* **Account Information:** Name, email address, phone number, password, profile picture, and account role (Job Seeker / Employer / Agency).
* **Professional Profile & Resume:** Current job role, headline, work history, education, skills, preferred job locations, portfolio links, and uploaded resume/CV documents.
* **Employer & Company Details:** Business name, organization ID, company logo, office photos/videos, verification documents, and job vacancy postings.
* **Communications:** Messages sent via in-app chat, recruiter feedback, job applications, questionnaire answers, and support requests.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
* **Device & Technical Information:** Device model, operating system, unique device identifiers, IP address, and push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
* **Usage & Engagement Data:** Job searches, saved jobs, connection requests, profile views, post interactions, and feed preferences.

### C. Device Permissions Requested
* **Camera Access:** To capture profile photos, company media, or verification documents.
* **Photo Library / Storage:** To select and upload resumes, documents, and media files.
* **Notifications:** To deliver instant alerts for job application updates, interview invites, connection requests, and direct messages.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
* **Job Matching & Networking:** Connecting job seekers with relevant job openings, mutual connections, and recruiters based on shared skills and network affinity.
* **Application Processing:** Submitting candidate resumes and profile applications directly to hiring managers and recruiting agencies.
* **Platform Security & Verification:** Verifying recruiter eligibility, issuing blue tick verification badges, and preventing fraud or policy violations.
* **Service Personalization:** Customizing your home feed with targeted job listings, company updates, and network recommendations.
* **Payments & Monetization:** Processing transactions for premium features, profile boosts, and job sponsorships via secure payment gateways (e.g., Razorpay).
* **Advertising & Communications:** Displaying relevant job ads (via Google AdMob) and sending operational updates, email notifications, and security alerts.

---

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We do **not** sell your personal data to third parties. We share your information only in the following operational circumstances:
* **With Employers & Recruiters:** When you apply for a job vacancy or set your profile as open to work, hiring managers can view your submitted details and resume.
* **With Other Users:** Public profile information, shared posts, and connections are visible to other members of the Hoxfor professional network according to your privacy settings.
* **Service Providers & Sub-processors:**
  * **Firebase (Google):** For authentication, Cloud Firestore database, Cloud Storage, and FCM push notifications.
  * **Razorpay:** For processing secure in-app billing transactions.
  * **Google AdMob:** For displaying relevant in-app advertising.
* **Legal & Safety Disclosures:** If required by law, subpoena, or to protect the safety, rights, and security of Hoxfor, our users, or the public.

---

## 4. Data Security & Retention

* **Data Protection:** We implement industry-standard encryption (TLS/HTTPS in transit and AES encryption at rest) and strict access controls via Firebase Security Rules.
* **Data Retention:** We retain your personal information as long as your account remains active. Upon account deletion, your personal profile and job application records are securely purged or anonymized, except where legal retention is required.

---

## 5. Your Rights & Choices

You have the following controls over your data:
* **Access & Edit:** Update your profile, resume, skills, and account preferences directly within the app settings.
* **Account Deletion:** Request permanent account deletion via **Settings > Account > Delete Account** or by contacting support.
* **Notification Preferences:** Turn push notifications or email alerts on or off anytime in **Settings > Notifications**.
* **Permissions Control:** Revoke camera, photo library, or notification permissions through your mobile device settings.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Hoxfor is designed for professionals and job seekers who are **16 years of age or older**. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If we discover a user under 16 has registered, we will promptly delete their account.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and notify users via in-app notifications or email for material updates.
