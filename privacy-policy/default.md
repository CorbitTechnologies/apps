# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 24, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 24, 2026  

This Privacy Policy describes how **Corbit Technologies** ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile and web applications (the "App" or "Services"), available on the Google Play Store, Apple App Store, and the web.

By downloading, accessing, or using our App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, maintain, and improve our applications. The types of data we collect include:

#### A. Information You Provide Directly
* **Account Information:** When you register, sign in, or interact with our App, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, and authentication credentials.
* **User Content:** Any profile information, images, documents, test reports, or feedback you upload directly through the App.
* **Communications:** Information provided when you contact customer support or communicate with our team.

#### B. Information Collected Automatically
* **Device Information:** Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (such as Device ID or UUID), and network provider information.
* **Log & Diagnostic Data:** IP address, access dates and times, app crashes, system activity, and performance metrics.
* **Push Notification Tokens:** Device push tokens (e.g., Firebase Cloud Messaging or Apple Push Notification Service tokens) to deliver critical updates and alerts.

#### C. Information from Third-Party Logins
If you sign in using third-party services (such as **Google Sign-In** or **Apple Sign-In**), we collect your name, email address, and authentication token as permitted by your third-party account settings.

### 2. Device Permissions We May Request

Depending on the specific features used in the App, we may request the following permissions:
* **Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS):** To deliver real-time task alerts, milestone updates, and critical system notifications.
* **Camera & Photo Library (CAMERA / READ_MEDIA_IMAGES):** To allow you to upload profile pictures, scan receipts, or attach reference images/screenshots.
* **Storage / Files:** To download generated vouchers, reports, or PDF documents locally to your device.

You can modify or revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings.

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
* To create and manage your user account and verify your identity.
* To provide, operate, and maintain the App's core functionality.

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. However, please note that no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

#### A. Data Retention
We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

#### B. Account & Data Deletion (User Rights)
In accordance with Google Play and Apple App Store policies, you have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time:
**In-App:** You can initiate account deletion via the **Settings ➔ Account ➔ Delete Account** section within the App.

Upon receiving your request, we will permanently delete or anonymize your personal data within thirty (30) days, except where retention is required by law.
