# Privacy Policy for Poyago

**Last Updated:** September 14, 2026

**Corbit Technologies** ("we," "our," or "us") operates the **Poyago** mobile application and platform (the "App"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App as a traveler/user (discovering and booking destinations, activities, and tour experiences) or as a partner/operator (listing attractions, activities, and guiding services).

By downloading, installing, or using Poyago, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

To provide a seamless, secure, and personalized destination discovery and travel booking experience, we collect several types of information:

### Personal Data
When you register an account, make a reservation, or apply as a partner/guide, we may collect personally identifiable information, including:
- **For Travelers/Users:** Full name, phone number, email address, profile photo, emergency contacts, and saved booking details.
- **For Tour Operators, Hosts & Activity Partners:** Full name, phone number, email address, agency or business name, profile/business photo, business location/address, bank account or payout details, and government-issued identification (ID) uploads for partner identity verification.

### Location Data
To recommend nearby attractions, showcase destination maps, provide accurate directions, and verify activity meeting spots, we collect:
- Precise or approximate geographical location (latitude and longitude) of your device, subject to your explicit permission.

### Transaction and Payment Data
To facilitate ticket bookings, reservations, and activity passes, we collect details related to your orders:
- Destination tickets, selected time slots, participant count, custom instructions, transaction amounts, and booking confirmation status.
- We utilize trusted, secure third-party payment processors (such as **Razorpay**). We do not store sensitive credit card credentials or complete banking information on our servers.

### Photos and Media Uploads
If you choose to upload photos for reviews, profile avatars, destination recommendations, or partner verification documents, we access your device camera and photo library only with your explicit permission.

### Usage and Device Data
We automatically collect diagnostic, device, and usage logs when you interact with the App:
- Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, network state, and IP address.
- In-app activity logs, destinations browsed, search queries, screen views, and crash reports.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for various operational and customer experience purposes, including to:
- Provide, operate, and maintain the Poyago platform for discovering and booking travel destinations, adventures, and activities.
- Determine your geographical location to showcase attractions, nearby experiences, and local events.
- Verify the identity and credentials of tour partners and guides to maintain high quality and safety standards.
- Process booking reservations, manage tickets, confirm payments, and coordinate meeting points.
- Calculate and disburse payouts and earnings to partner agencies and guides.
- Deliver push notifications, booking confirmations, itinerary updates, and customer support.
- Detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, and security vulnerabilities.

---

## 3. How Your Information Is Shared

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or transactional data to third parties. We share information only under the following circumstances:
- **Between Travelers and Activity Partners/Guides:** To fulfill confirmed reservations, necessary details (such as traveler name, contact number, and booking time slot) are shared with the respective destination host or tour operator.
- **With Service Providers:** We share data with reliable third-party infrastructure and service providers (including **Firebase / Google Cloud** for database and authentication, **Razorpay** for payment processing, and SMS/OTP verification services) to maintain and deliver the App.
- **For Legal and Compliance Reasons:** We may disclose your information if required to do so by applicable law, court order, or governmental regulation, or in good faith belief that such disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, prevent fraud, or protect the personal safety of our users.
- **Business Transfers:** In the event of a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, user records may be transferred as a business asset subject to standard confidentiality protections.

---

## 4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures—including encrypted transmission (HTTPS/TLS) and restricted database access—to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no electronic transmission or storage method over the internet is completely impenetrable, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal, booking, and partner verification data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes set forth in this policy and to satisfy any legal, tax, accounting, or reporting obligations.

You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time. You can initiate account deletion within the App settings (Profile > Delete Account) or by emailing your request to our support team at the contact address below. Upon confirmation, your data will be permanently deleted or anonymized from our active databases.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Poyago is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such information from our records.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our services, technology, or legal requirements. When updates occur, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy and notify users through an in-app notice or other appropriate communication channels.
