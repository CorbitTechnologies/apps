# Privacy Policy for Back2Track

**Last Updated: June 27, 2026**

Corbit Technologies ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Back2Track mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App. 

By downloading, installing, or using Back2Track, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

To provide a personalized health and nutrition tracking experience, we may collect several types of information:

### Personal Data
When you register an account or interact with the App, you may provide personally identifiable information, including but not limited to:
*   Name or username
*   Email address
*   Profile picture

### Health and Fitness Data
To calculate your calorie and macronutrient targets (e.g., proteins, carbs, fats), we may collect and process health-related information that you voluntarily log, such as:
*   Daily food consumption, calories, and nutrient intake
*   Activity logs, streaks, and milestone achievements
*   Weight, height, fitness goals, and target metrics

### Usage and Device Data
We may automatically collect certain information when you access the App, including:
*   Your device's IP address, device type, operating system, and unique device identifiers
*   In-app activity logs, features accessed, and page views
*   App crash reports and performance diagnostics

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for various purposes, including to:
*   Provide, maintain, and improve the core functionalities of Back2Track
*   Calculate and display personalized calorie and macronutrient goals
*   Manage your user account, daily rewards, points, and streaks
*   Analyze usage patterns to improve the user interface and app performance
*   Respond to your support requests, inquiries, or feedback
*   Send you essential administrative notifications regarding your account or policy updates

*   **For Legal Reasons:** We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, or in the good faith belief that such action is necessary to comply with legal obligations or protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or the public.

If you wish to delete your account or request the permanent deletion of your stored data, you may do so directly through the App's settings under your Profile, or by contacting us using the details provided below.
