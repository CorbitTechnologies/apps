# Privacy Policy for Smartq

**Last Updated:** September 9, 2026

**Corbit Technologies** ("we", "our", or "us") operates the **SmartqHospital** mobile application (the "Service" / "App"). This Privacy Policy explains our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our hospital queue and management services:

### A. Personal & Account Information
- **Account Credentials:** Email address, username, password (encrypted), and role designation (e.g., Doctor, Receptionist, Admin).
- **Hospital Identifier (HID):** A unique hospital identification code used to link authorized personnel and patients to the appropriate healthcare facility.
- **Staff / Profile Details:** Name, department, specialization, contact information, and shift availability.
- **Patient Queue Information:** Patient token numbers, names, ages, contact numbers, and appointment statuses entered by hospital staff for queue and consultation management.

### B. Usage & Device Data
- **Device Information:** Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and network information.
- **Log Data:** Information regarding interactions within the app, such as login timestamps, token status changes, and error logs to ensure platform reliability.

### C. Local Storage
- We use local storage (`SharedPreferences`) on your device to store preferences such as your Hospital ID (HID) and session state to keep you signed in securely.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
- **Queue & Token Management:** Facilitating patient queueing, token generation, doctor consultations, and real-time status updates.
- **Authentication & Access Control:** Verifying staff credentials and restricting unauthorized access to sensitive hospital data.
- **Hospital Communication & Workflow:** Enabling smooth coordination between receptionists, doctors, administrators, and patients.
- **App Maintenance & Improvement:** Monitoring system performance, diagnosing bugs, and enhancing user experience.
- **Customer Support:** Responding to inquiries, feedback, and technical support requests.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services and Data Processors

We use trusted third-party service providers to support our backend infrastructure and database storage:

- **Google Firebase Services:**
  - **Firebase Authentication:** For secure user sign-in and identity verification.
  - **Cloud Firestore & Firebase Realtime Database:** For real-time, encrypted storage and synchronization of queue data, tokens, and staff records.

These third-party providers have their own privacy policies addressing how they handle data. You can review Google's privacy policy at [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your data. All communication between the app and our Firebase backend is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect personal data, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

- **Retention:** We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide hospital queue services or comply with legal obligations.
- **Account & Data Deletion:** Users and hospital administrators may request the deletion of their accounts and associated data at any time by contacting us at **support@corbittechnologies.com**. Upon receipt of a verified request, data will be permanently deleted within 30 days, subject to applicable legal requirements.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Our Service is intended for healthcare facility operations and authorized personnel. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information directly from children under 13 without verified parental or legal guardian consent provided through the healthcare provider. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take immediate action to remove the information.
