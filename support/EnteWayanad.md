## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. How do I add my shop, service, or business listing?
* Open the **Ente Wayanad** app.
* Navigate to your relevant category or go to **Profile / Register Business**.
* Fill in your business details (Name, Contact Number, Panchayat/Town, Description, and Photos).
* Submit your listing. Our team reviews submissions to ensure data accuracy and quality.

### 2. How can I edit or delete my listing?
* If you created a listing using your registered phone number, you can manage it from the **My Listings** section in your profile.
* Alternatively, email us at [support@wayanadcity.com](mailto:support@wayanadcity.com) with your listing name and registered phone number, and our support team will update or remove it for you.

### 3. How do I report incorrect numbers or outdated listings?
* Tap on the listing inside the app and select the **Report Issue** option.
* You can also email us directly at [support@wayanadcity.com](mailto:support@wayanadcity.com) with the listing name and the correction.

### 4. How do I request account and data deletion?
* If you wish to delete your account, saved bookmarks, or associated personal information:
  * Send an email to [support@wayanadcity.com](mailto:support@wayanadcity.com) with the subject **"Account Deletion Request"** from or mentioning your registered phone number.
  * Your account and all associated data will be permanently removed from our servers within 7 business days.

### 5. App Requirements:
* **Android:** Requires Android 6.0 (Marshmallow) or higher.
* **iOS:** Requires iOS 15.0 or higher (iPhone & iPad).
