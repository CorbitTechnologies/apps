## Getting Started

### System Requirements

**iOS**
- iOS 12.0 or later
- iPhone or iPad
- Minimum 100MB free storage
- Active internet connection (for sync)

**Android**
- Android 8.0 or later
- Minimum 100MB free storage
- Active internet connection (for sync)

### Installation

1. Download Phinverse Branch Tracker from the App Store or Google Play
2. Open the app and sign in with your company credentials
3. Accept the permissions (location, notifications, camera)
4. Complete your profile setup
5. You're ready to start tracking!

### Initial Setup

1. **Verify Your Account**: Confirm your email and phone number
2. **Set Permissions**: Grant necessary app permissions
3. **Configure Notifications**: Choose your alert preferences
4. **Add Your Branch**: If you're a manager, set up your branch location
5. **Invite Team Members**: Add staff to your team

## Account & Login

### How do I reset my password?

1. On the login screen, tap **"Forgot Password?"**
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your email for a reset link (check spam folder)
4. Click the link and create a new password
5. Log in with your new password

### How do I enable two-factor authentication (2FA)?

1. Go to **Settings** > **Security**
2. Tap **Two-Factor Authentication**
3. Select your preferred method:
   - SMS (text message)
   - Email verification
   - Authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator)
4. Follow the prompts to verify
5. Save backup codes in a secure location

### How do I update my profile?

1. Tap the **Profile icon** (top-right corner)
2. Tap **Edit Profile**
3. Update your information:
   - Name
   - Phone number
   - Profile photo
   - Job title
   - Department
4. Tap **Save**

### How do I change my notification settings?

1. Go to **Settings** > **Notifications**
2. Toggle notifications for:
   - Performance alerts
   - Task assignments
   - Compliance notices
   - Status updates
   - General notifications
3. Set quiet hours (optional)
4. Changes save automatically

### What should I do if I suspect unauthorized access?

1. **Change your password immediately**
2. **Enable 2FA** if not already enabled
3. **Review login activity** in Settings > Security > Login History
4. **Contact IT Support** at security@phinversefinance.com
5. Report any suspicious activity

## Branch Management

### How do I add a new branch?

**For Administrators Only:**
1. Go to **Branches** > **Add Branch**
2. Enter branch details:
   - Branch name
   - Location (address)
   - Manager name
   - Opening hours
   - Contact number
3. Tap **Create Branch**
4. Share branch code with managers

### How do I update branch information?

1. Select the branch from the **Branches** list
2. Tap **Edit** (pencil icon)
3. Update the required fields:
   - Location
   - Hours of operation
   - Contact information
   - Manager details
4. Tap **Save**

### How do I view branch performance?

1. Go to **Dashboard** or **Analytics**
2. Select your branch from the dropdown
3. View key metrics:
   - Revenue/Performance
   - Staff count
   - Compliance status
   - Recent activities
4. Swipe to see detailed charts and trends

### How do I export branch reports?

1. Select a branch
2. Go to **Reports** > **Branch Report**
3. Choose report type:
   - Daily Report
   - Weekly Report
   - Monthly Report
   - Custom Date Range
4. Tap **Export** > Select format (PDF/Excel)
5. Report downloads to your device

### How do I view branch locations on a map?

1. Go to **Locations** or **Map View**
2. View all branch locations on an interactive map
3. Tap a branch pin to see details
4. Swipe to access branch profile

## Team Management

### How do I add team members?

1. Go to **Team** > **Add Member**
2. Enter employee details:
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - Phone number
   - Job title
   - Department

3. Select branch assignment
4. Tap **Send Invite**

1. Go to **Team** > Select employee
2. Tap **Edit Permissions**
3. Choose role:

   - Manager (branch management)

### How do I track employee performance?

1. Go to **Team** > Select employee
2. View performance metrics:

1. Go to **Team** > Select employee

### How do I track attendance?

1. Go to **Attendance**

## Troubleshooting

### App won't open or keeps crashing

**Solution:**
1. Force close the app:
   - iOS: Swipe up from app switcher
   - Android: Settings > Apps > Force Close
2. Restart your device
3. Clear app cache:
   - iOS: Settings > General > iPhone Storage > Phinverse > Offload App
   - Android: Settings > Apps > Phinverse > Storage > Clear Cache
4. Reinstall the app if issue persists

### Login issues

**"Invalid credentials" error:**
1. Verify your email/username spelling
2. Check CAPS LOCK is off
3. Try resetting your password
4. Clear app cache and try again

**"Account locked" message:**
1. Wait 30 minutes before retrying
2. Contact IT Support to unlock account
3. Verify your identity with security questions

**Can't receive reset emails:**
1. Check spam/junk folder
2. Add support@phinversefinance.com to contacts
3. Try alternative email if available
4. Contact IT Support

### Sync issues - data not updating

**Solution:**
1. Check internet connection (WiFi or cellular)
2. Go to **Settings** > **Sync**
3. Tap **Manual Sync** or **Sync Now**
4. Wait for sync to complete
5. If still not syncing:
   - Force close app
   - Restart device
   - Reopen app
   - Try syncing again

### Location not showing correctly

**Solution:**
1. Go to **Settings** > **Permissions** > **Location**
2. Ensure location permission is **"Always"** or **"While Using App"**
3. Turn on device location services:
   - iOS: Settings > Privacy > Location Services > ON
   - Android: Settings > Location > ON
4. Try disabling and re-enabling location
5. Clear app cache and restart

### Notifications not working

**Solution:**
1. Go to **Settings** > **Notifications**
2. Verify notifications are enabled
3. Check device notification settings:
   - iOS: Settings > Notifications > Phinverse > Allow Notifications
   - Android: Settings > Apps > Phinverse > Notifications > ON
4. Disable and re-enable notifications
5. Restart app

### Slow performance

**Solution:**
1. Close other running apps
2. Restart your device
3. Clear app cache
4. Reduce number of branches/data visible
5. Check available storage (need at least 100MB free)
6. Update app to latest version
7. Check internet connection speed

### Map not loading

**Solution:**
1. Check internet connection
2. Verify location permission granted
3. Disable and enable location services
4. Clear app cache
5. Update app to latest version
6. Restart device

### Export not working

**Solution:**
1. Check available storage space
2. Verify file permissions:
   - iOS: Settings > Phinverse > Files & iCloud
   - Android: Settings > Phinverse > Storage
3. Try different export format
4. Restart app and try again
5. Clear app cache

### Can't add team members

**Solution:**
1. Verify you have admin permissions
2. Check employee email address is correct
3. Ensure employee isn't already in system
4. Try inviting again after 1 minute
5. Contact IT Support if persistent

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is my data secure?

Yes. All data is encrypted with military-grade encryption both in transit and at rest. We follow enterprise security standards and undergo regular security audits.

### Can I use the app offline?

Partial offline functionality is available. You can view cached data, but new entries will sync when you reconnect to the internet.

### How often is data backed up?

Data is automatically backed up every 6 hours. Critical changes are backed up immediately.

### Can I delete my account?

Contact IT Support to request account deletion. Your historical data will be archived per company policy.

### What's the maximum team size?

No limit. The app handles thousands of team members across multiple branches.

### Can I use multiple accounts?

No. One account per employee. Contact IT Support if you need to switch accounts.

### How do I request new features?

Go to **Settings** > **Feedback** or email support@phinversefinance.com with your suggestion.

### Are there video tutorials?

Visit our internal knowledge base or YouTube channel (if available) for video guides.

### What's the data retention policy?

Active data is retained indefinitely. Inactive accounts are archived after 12 months. Contact IT Support for data requests.

### Can I schedule tasks for future?

Yes. When creating tasks, set the due date to any future date. Reminders will notify assigned users.
