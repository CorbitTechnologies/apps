## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 🎓 Courses & Learning Material

#### How do I enrol in a course?
1. Open the Lawtop app and navigate to the **Home** tab.
2. Scroll to find a course you are interested in or tap **My Courses** to view your enrolled list.
3. Tap on the course card to view the description, syllabus, and sample materials.
4. Tap **Enroll Free** or complete the premium checkout process to get instant access.

#### Can I view study materials and PDFs offline?
Yes! Once you open any PDF study guide or previous year solved question paper inside the app, it is automatically cached on your device. You can access and read your downloaded study materials anytime without an active internet connection.

#### What should I do if a course video isn't playing?
If a video lecture fails to load or stream:
* Ensure you have a stable internet connection (minimum 2 Mbps recommended for high-definition streaming).
* Try restarting the video or relaunching the application.
* Go to **Profile > App Settings** and clear the app cache, then try again.

### 📝 Mock Tests & Exam Prep

#### How do mock tests work?
Mock tests are designed to simulate real exam environments:
* Tap on **My Tests** or choose a test under your preferred exam category.
* Review the test duration, question count, and instruction guidelines.
* Tap **Start Test** to begin. The timer will start automatically.
* Once completed, tap **Submit** to view your score, detailed performance analytics, and correct answers with expert explanations.

#### Can I pause and resume a mock test?
No. To maintain exam integrity and prepare you effectively for real-world testing environments, mock tests must be completed in one sitting once started.

### 💳 Transactions & Purchases

#### How do I restore my purchases?
If you have reinstalled the app or switched to a new device:
1. Ensure you are signed in with the **same account** (Google, Apple, or Email) used to make the purchase.
2. Navigate to **Profile > Enrolled Courses** or **My Tests**.
3. Tap the **Restore Purchases** button at the bottom of the screen.
4. Your active enrolments and premium mock tests will be instantly restored.


## 🛠️ App Troubleshooting & Updates

* **Keep your app updated:** Ensure you are always running the latest version of Lawtop from the App Store to receive performance enhancements, bug fixes, and new features.
* **Network Connectivity:** Lawtop requires an active internet connection for streaming video content, starting mock tests, and syncing your progress.
* **Storage Space:** Ensure your device has at least 100MB of free space to successfully download and cache offline study materials.
