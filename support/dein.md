## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. How do I explore interior and exterior products?

Browse the available categories from the home screen, such as kitchens, bedrooms, living rooms, wardrobes, furniture, and exterior solutions. Select a product or design to view its details, specifications, pricing, and available options.

### 2. How can I request an estimate or enquiry?

Open the product or design you are interested in and use the **Estimate** or **Enquire Now** option. Provide the required measurements and project details, if requested. Our team or associated professionals will review your enquiry and contact you.

### 3. How are measurements and prices calculated?

Product pricing may be based on measurements such as square feet, running feet, units, or other applicable dimensions. The app provides measurement guidance where available. Final pricing may vary based on selected materials, specifications, measurements, installation, and other project requirements.

### 4. Can I save or manage products I am interested in?

Yes. Depending on the available features, you can save, shortlist, or add products and designs to your selected list for easier reference and enquiry.

### 5. How do I report incorrect product information?

If you find incorrect pricing, measurements, product details, images, or other information, please contact us at [support@corbittechnologies.com](mailto:support@corbittechnologies.com). Include the product name and details of the issue so our team can review it.

### 6. Account and Data Privacy

To request account deletion or data removal, please email [support@corbittechnologies.com](mailto:support@corbittechnologies.com) from your registered email address with the subject line **"Account Deletion Request"**.
