## Frequently Asked Questions
### 1. How do I book a cab?
Navigate to the **Cab Booking** section from the home screen or explore categories. Select your pickup location, drop-off location, date, time, and preferred vehicle type (Mini, Sedan, Minivan), then submit your inquiry. Our local travel partners will confirm your booking.
### 2. Can I cancel or reschedule my inquiry?
Yes. To reschedule or cancel an inquiry, please contact our support team at [support@corbittechnologies.com](mailto:support@corbittechnologies.com) with your registered phone number and booking details.
### 3. How do I report a listing or incorrect information?
If you notice outdated bus timings, incorrect listing prices, or inappropriate content, please email us with the listing title and details. Our moderation team reviews all reports promptly.
### 4. Account and Data Privacy
To request account deletion or data removal, please email [support@corbittechnologies.com](mailto:support@corbittechnologies.com) from your registered email address with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
