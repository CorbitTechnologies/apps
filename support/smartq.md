## Getting Started

### System Requirements

**iOS**
- iOS 13.0 or later
- iPhone or iPad
- Active internet connection

**Android**
- Android 8.0 or later
- Active internet connection

### Installation

1. Download SmartQ Hospital from the App Store or Google Play
2. Open the app and wait for the preload screen to finish loading
3. You will be directed to the login screen
4. Ensure you have your hospital credentials ready

### Initial Setup

1. **Verify Your Account**: Ensure your hospital administrator has set up your account.
2. **First Login**: Enter your credentials on the login screen.
3. **Role Selection**: Depending on your permissions, you will be prompted to select your role (e.g., Doctor, Receptionist).
4. **Hospital Setup**: If you are an administrator logging in for the first time, you may need to complete the hospital setup process.

## Account & Login

### How do I log in?

1. Open the app to the **Login Screen**.
2. Enter your registered email address and password.
3. Tap **Login**.
4. If your account or hospital has been deactivated, you will be redirected to an informational screen and will need to contact your administrator.

### What if my account is deactivated?

If you see the "Account Deactivated" or "Hospital Disabled" screen upon logging in, your access has been temporarily suspended by the hospital administrator or system admin. Please contact your internal IT or HR department to restore access.

## Role Selection

### How do I switch roles?

If your account has multiple permissions (e.g., you act as both a Receptionist and a Doctor), you will be presented with the **Role Selection Screen** after logging in. 
1. Tap on the role you are fulfilling for the current shift.
2. You will be directed to the corresponding dashboard.

## Receptionist Guide

### How do I manage the waiting room?

The **Receptionist Home Screen** is your main dashboard.
1. View the live overview of all active queues.
2. Monitor patient wait times and current queue lengths.

### How do I add a patient to the queue?

1. From the Receptionist Home Screen, tap **Add to Queue**.
2. Enter the patient's details (Name, Contact Info, Reason for Visit).
3. Select the appropriate department or doctor they are waiting to see.
4. Tap **Confirm** to place them in the smart queue.

---

## Doctor Guide

### How do I view my patients?

The **Doctor Home Screen** displays your personal queue.
1. You can see the list of patients assigned to you in order.
2. The screen shows their wait times and basic triage information.

### How do I start a consultation?

1. Select the next patient in your queue from the Doctor Home Screen.
2. Tap to open the **Consultation Page**.
3. Here, you can review patient notes, add your own medical notes, and complete the consultation.
4. Once finished, mark the consultation as complete to automatically call the next patient in the queue.

## Queue Management

### How do patients select their queue?

If patients are using a self-service kiosk or the patient-facing side of the app:
1. They will see the **Patient Queue Selection Screen**.
2. They can select the department or specific doctor they need to see.
3. The system will automatically generate a queue number and estimated wait time for them.

## Troubleshooting

### App is stuck on the Preload Screen

**Solution:**
1. Check your internet connection (WiFi or Cellular).
2. Force close the app and reopen it.
3. If the issue persists, clear the app cache or reinstall the app.

### I can't see the patient in my queue

**Solution:**
1. Pull down on your home screen to refresh the data.
2. Verify with the receptionist that the patient was assigned to your specific queue and not a general department queue.
3. Ensure you have a stable internet connection for real-time updates.

### Login issues

**"Invalid credentials" error:**
1. Verify your email spelling.
2. Ensure you are using the correct password provided by your hospital administrator.

## Contact Support

### How to Contact SmartQ Support

**Email Support**
- Email: support@smartqhospital.com
- Include: Your Hospital Name, Device type, and a description of the issue.

**IT Help Desk**
- For internal hospital issues (deactivated accounts, incorrect roles), please contact your hospital's internal IT administrator directly.

### Report a Bug

If you experience unexpected behavior:
1. Note what you were trying to do.
2. Note the steps to reproduce the issue.
3. Email our support team with the details and any screenshots.
